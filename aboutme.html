About Pawfect

Millions of animals go missing every year, but many are never reunited with their families simply because there's no easy and universal way to report or find them. At the same time, countless stray animals are looking for a home, just as many people are searching for a pet to love.

This platform was created after we saw so many lost animal posts online and witnessed stray animals in our own neighborhoods, with no real solution to help them. We knew technology could make a difference. We care deeply about animals, and this project allows us to use tech in a meaningful way to reconnect families and offer hope to animals still waiting for one.
